CareerPulse Documentation

1. App Overview

CareerPulse is a premium career tracking platform built specifically for job seekers on Android devices. It enables users to record job applications, track recruitment progress across interactive timelines, configure smart job alert monitors, analyze performance metrics, and calculate AI job match compatibility scores.

Supported Platform: Android (v1.0.0)

2. Installation Guide

  1. Click the Download APK button on the landing page or scroll to:
  2. Once downloaded, locate CareerPulse.apk in your Android device's Downloads folder.
  3. Tap the file to initiate installation. If prompted, enable "Install Unknown Apps" in your Android Security Settings.
  4. Once installed, tap the CareerPulse app icon on your home screen or app drawer to launch.

3. Login & Subscription Guide

  1. Navigate to the Login / Subscription card on the website or open the CareerPulse mobile application.
  2. Enter your active Robi or Airtel mobile number (e.g. 018XXXXXXXX or 016XXXXXXXX).
  3. Tap Continue to request a 6-digit OTP via SMS.
  4. Enter the received 6-digit OTP code into the verification input field and tap Verify & Subscribe.
  5. Upon successful verification, your daily micro-subscription of 2.78 BDT (including VAT + SC + SD) will activate and you will be redirected to your dashboard.

4. Usage Guide

  • Dashboard & Timeline: View your active job applications sorted by deadline. Track application status (Saved, Applied, Interviewing).
  • Smart Alerts: Create and toggle keyword-driven job search alerts for continuous monitoring.
  • Analytics: Review weekly application trends and response metrics in interactive line graphs.

5. Subscription Information

Daily Subscription Rate: 2.78 BDT (including VAT + SC + SD)

Supported Operators: Robi and Airtel subscribers only.

Billing Cycle: Daily auto-renewable subscription billed via mobile airtime balance.

Renewal: Automatically renews daily unless explicitly unsubscribed by the user.

6. Logout & Unsubscription Instructions

Logging Out: Click the Logout icon in the top header menu. Logging out signs you out of the portal while preserving your subscription.

Unsubscribing: Click the "Unsubscribe" button in the top navigation bar when logged in, or follow the BDApps operator USSD/SMS cancellation instructions.
